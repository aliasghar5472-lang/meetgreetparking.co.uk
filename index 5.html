Cheap parking for Luton Airport Parkway

Leave your car in our town centre lot, walk two minutes to Luton station interchange and carry on to Luton Airport Parkway. Prices start at £7 a day.

Staying a week or more? Our weekly rate is £45 and our monthly rate is £120.

We collect and return your car at the airport

Flying from Luton Airport? Hand over your car at the airport and we park it in our lot. When you land, we bring it back to you.

Tick the airport service when you book. Our team will contact you with a price, between £7 and £15, paid to our driver in cash or by bank transfer. Nothing extra to pay online.

Collection and return: £7 to £15, quoted by our team

How it works

Three steps, all from your phone.

  1. Pick your dates

    Choose your arrival day and how many days you need, from 1 to 30. The price updates as you go.

  2. Pay online

    Enter your details and pay by card. You get a confirmation with your dates and registration.

  3. Park and go

    Park in the lot and walk two minutes to Luton station interchange for Luton Airport Parkway, or add our airport collection and return service.

Right by Luton station

The lot is in front of Luton town centre, a two-minute walk from Luton station interchange. From there, you can continue to Luton Airport Parkway.

Questions

Anything else, call or email us.

How many days can I book?

From one day up to 30 days online. For longer stays, contact us.

How is my price worked out?

We take the cheapest of the daily rate (£7), the weekly rate (£45) and the monthly rate (£120) for your stay. For example, 6 days costs £42, 7 days costs £45, 10 days costs £66, and any stay from 19 to 30 days costs £120.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Contact us before your arrival date and we will help. Please quote your vehicle registration.

How do I get to the airport?

Walk two minutes to Luton station interchange and continue to Luton Airport Parkway.
