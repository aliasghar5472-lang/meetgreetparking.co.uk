How it works

Three steps, all from your phone.

  1. Pick your dates

    Choose your arrival day and how many days you need, from 1 to 30. The price updates as you go.

  2. Pay online

    Enter your details and pay by card. You get a confirmation with your dates and registration.

  3. Park and go

    Park in the lot and walk two minutes to Luton station interchange for Luton Airport Parkway, or add our taxi to Luton Airport Parkway for £40 return.

Questions

Anything else, call or email us.

How many days can I book?

From one day up to 30 days online. For longer stays, contact us.

How is my price worked out?

We take the cheapest of the daily rate (£7), the weekly rate (£45) and the monthly rate (£120) for your stay. For example, 6 days costs £42, 7 days costs £45, 10 days costs £66, and any stay from 19 to 30 days costs £120.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Contact us before your arrival date and we will help. Please quote your vehicle registration.

How do I get to the airport?

Walk two minutes to Luton station interchange and continue to Luton Airport Parkway. Or add our taxi from the lot to Luton Airport Parkway, about a 10 minute drive, for £40 there and back.
